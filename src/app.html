EXIF has no film field. The stock goes into UserComment, and optionally ImageDescription — Lightroom shows that as the Caption, so it's searchable. Set ISO to your push/pull speed if you rated it differently.

This browser can't save back into folders. After writing you'll get  Save to Photos / Share  (on phones) or a ZIP. On a computer, Chrome, Edge or Arc can save straight into the folder.
Open a folder of scans or drop it here. JPEG and TIFF files are listed in filename order; nothing is written until you press Write.
Everything runs locally in this page — no uploads. JPEG image data is copied byte-for-byte (no recompression); TIFFs get a new tag directory appended. Existing tags you leave blank are kept. Embedded EXIF thumbnails and maker notes in JPEGs are dropped.
